Pilot 03 · Common Grounds

A rooftop that works on every level.

Common Grounds is my side business: coffee, pickleball and padel on a covered rooftop, open from six in the morning to two at night. It is also the most successful thing I run.

Status OpenHours 6 am – 2 amResults in my letter

The bet

Build for the whole ladder, not just the roof.

HypothesisA space that serves people on all five levels of Maslow's ladder gets used all day, and keeps them coming back.

I think Common Grounds works because of how it's built. A space in a city shouldn't just give people a roof. It should serve them all the way up, from a coffee to becoming someone who shows up for themselves every week.

It's the same thing I believe about buildings, on a rooftop small enough to test in months instead of years.
