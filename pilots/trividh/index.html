Pilot 01 · Trividh

Still watering its gardens when the city runs dry.

Trividh, by House of Cosmos, is an art deco building in Naupada with a small water plant inside it. This page is where I keep score on the bet I am making with it.

Status Under constructionHandover 2028Results in my letter

The bet

Pay on day one for water saved in year eight.

HypothesisA building can clean and reuse 90% of its own grey and rainwater.

A water plant inside the building costs more to build. Nobody pays a developer for water that will be saved years after handover, so most buildings skip it. Trividh doesn't.

If the bet holds, the people living here use less water from the city, call fewer tankers in the dry months, and still have water for their gardens when the taps around them run short.
